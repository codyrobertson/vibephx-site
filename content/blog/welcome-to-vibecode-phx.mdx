We're excited to launch **Vibe Code Phoenix**, a community-driven platform for developers, designers, and tech enthusiasts in the Phoenix area.

## Our Mission

Our mission is simple: **build a thriving tech community** where people can:

- Learn new skills through hands-on workshops
- Network with fellow developers
- Share knowledge and experiences
- Collaborate on projects
- Grow their careers

## What We Offer

### Workshops
Regular hands-on workshops covering the latest technologies, frameworks, and best practices. Earn credits for attending!

### Events
Community meetups, hackathons, and networking events to connect with other professionals.

### Resources
Curated learning materials, tutorials, and guides to help you level up your skills.

## Get Involved

Ready to join? Check out our [upcoming workshops](/workshops) or explore our [resources](/resources) to start learning today!
